Our Story

Founded in 2019, ERA began as a passion project to bring unique, independent voices to the forefront of cinema. From our humble beginnings, we've grown into a premiere global creative development company dedicated to acquiring, producing, and distributing indie films that tell compelling stories and captivate audiences worldwide. Our journey is rooted in a love for storytelling, where each film we touch becomes a part of a larger narrative of innovation, creativity, and cultural impact. Through challenges and triumphs, ERA has built a legacy of fostering emerging talent and delivering films that resonate on a deep, emotional level, pushing the boundaries of independent filmmaking.

Vision & Mission

At ERA, our vision is to champion the art of storytelling through independent cinema, creating films that inspire, challenge, and entertain. Our mission is to discover and nurture compelling indie narratives, guiding them from acquisition through production to global distribution, ensuring that diverse voices and innovative stories reach audiences everywhere.

Global Strategy

ERA operates as a vertically integrated creative development company, overseeing every step from acquiring promising scripts to producing captivating films and distributing them globally. We handle 3–5 indie projects annually, each crafted with bold narratives and exceptional talent to appeal to international audiences. Our worldwide network of creators and investors ensures a vibrant pipeline of stories and support.

Sustainability Initiatives

Committed to responsible filmmaking, ERA employs eco-friendly practices in our acquisition, production, and distribution processes. By using energy-efficient tools, optimizing logistics, and favoring digital methods, we reduce our environmental footprint while maintaining the integrity and quality of our indie stories.

Talent Acquisition

ERA partners with a diverse array of talented actors, directors, and writers to bring indie stories to life. We focus on collaborating with both seasoned professionals and rising stars to create authentic, resonant narratives that capture the essence of human experience and drive emotional engagement.

We've cultivated our own constellation of stars, blending established names with fresh talent to infuse our films with unique energy and perspective.

Technological Innovation

Embracing cutting-edge technology, ERA enhances our storytelling process. AI streamlines workflows, VR offers immersive previews, and blockchain secures our creative assets, all while preserving the artistic heart of our indie films.

Revenue Streams

- Theatrical Releases: Bringing our stories to big screens worldwide, with multiple releases each year.
- Streaming & VOD: Partnering with platforms to make our films accessible anytime, anywhere.
- International Sales: Licensing our narratives to global markets for broad reach.
- Merchandising & IP: Extending our stories into books, games, and merchandise.
- Grants & Sponsorships: Securing support from industry and private sources to fuel our creative endeavors.

Investment Opportunities

For Investors: ERA is a registered corporation strategically positioned to partner with accredited investors, maximizing our reach and impact in the indie film world. Our exceptional talent pool, including award-winning creators, opens doors to substantial financing through our global network. We offer attractive opportunities via equity and profit-sharing, backed by transparent blockchain tracking. Join us in shaping the future of independent storytelling and benefit from a diversified portfolio of compelling films and IP ventures.

Strategic Partnerships

ERA collaborates with leading platforms and tech innovators to amplify our stories' reach. These partnerships ensure our indie films gain maximum visibility while advancing our production and distribution capabilities.
