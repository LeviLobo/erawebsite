ERA RECORDING ARCHIVE

E – Era. Founded in 2019, we began in the raw, trembling hinge between a dying world and whatever wild thing comes next.

R – Recording. From that first fracture we have seized the unfiltered human stories: every cracked voice, every defiant heartbeat, every whisper that refused the dark.

A – Archive. We swear, with every future breath and fire we can steal, to carry these sparks across every night that tries to swallow them, refusing surrender, refusing silence.

Like Dylan Thomas said,
Do not go gentle into that good night.
Rage, rage against the dying of the light.

With that same fury we fight to keep the spark of humanity alive.
Forever.
ERA is that fight.
Born in 2019.
Never over.
